## 1. Price Sensitivity / Cost Complaints

### Case 1

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month contract, 4 months tenure, fiber internet + streaming bundle, no phone service.

**Flagged Reason:** Monthly charges in the top quartile relative to tenure; no long-term contract to anchor them.

**Action Taken:** Offered a 10% loyalty discount contingent on switching to a 1-year contract.

**Outcome:** Retained. Converted to 1-year contract, no churn signal in the following 3 months.

---

### Case 2

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 2 months tenure, DSL internet only, paperless billing, electronic check payment.

**Flagged Reason:** Very short tenure combined with a payment method associated with higher churn (electronic check) in this cohort.

**Action Taken:** Proactive call offering a bundled discount for adding online security.

**Outcome:** Churned anyway within 3 weeks. Exit reason recorded as "found cheaper provider" -- discount wasn't competitive enough.

---

### Case 3

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 8 months tenure, fiber + streaming TV + streaming movies, high monthly charges.

**Flagged Reason:** Multiple stacked premium add-ons driving cost complaints in support logs.

**Action Taken:** Rep walked customer through downgrading one streaming add-on instead of a blanket discount, preserving margin.

**Outcome:** Retained. Customer appreciated being shown a cheaper configuration rather than just a discount; stayed on month-to-month but usage stabilized.

---

### Case 4

**Customer Profile:** One-year contract, 11 months tenure (near renewal), DSL + phone service, no streaming.

**Flagged Reason:** Called in twice in one month asking about "cheaper plans."

**Action Taken:** Locked in a renewal rate matching their current price instead of the usual renewal increase.

**Outcome:** Retained. Renewed for another year at flat pricing.

---

### Case 5

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 1 month tenure, fiber internet, senior citizen.

**Flagged Reason:** New customer, high fiber pricing, no bundled discount applied at signup.

**Action Taken:** Initial senior-citizen bundle discount wasn't enough on its own -- customer said the fiber speed itself wasn't worth the base price. Rep escalated to a supervisor-approved deeper discount plus a downgrade to a lower fiber tier matching their actual usage.

**Outcome:** Retained. The combination of lower price and right-sized service tier addressed the real complaint instead of just discounting the same plan.

---

## 2. Poor Service Experience (Outages, Support Issues)

### Case 6

**Customer Profile:** Two-year contract, 14 months tenure, fiber internet, tech support add-on.

**Flagged Reason:** Three support tickets in 60 days, all related to intermittent outages.

**Action Taken:** Escalated to a technician dispatch instead of another phone troubleshooting call; also credited one month's bill.

**Outcome:** Retained. Root cause (faulty line) was fixed; no further tickets in 4 months.

---

### Case 7

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 6 months tenure, DSL, no tech support add-on.

**Flagged Reason:** One severe outage complaint escalated to a formal complaint email.

**Action Taken:** Offered a bill credit and a free month of tech support.

**Outcome:** Churned two weeks later. Customer said the outage itself (not the compensation) had already broken trust.

---

### Case 8

**Customer Profile:** One-year contract, 9 months tenure, fiber + device protection.

**Flagged Reason:** Repeated billing errors (three incorrect charges in a row) rather than a service outage.

**Action Taken:** Manual billing audit + apology credit + assigned a named account contact for future issues.

**Outcome:** Retained. Customer specifically cited "having a real person to call" as the reason they stayed.

---

### Case 9

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 3 months tenure, fiber, multiple lines.

**Flagged Reason:** Long hold times reported in a support satisfaction survey (low score, not yet churned).

**Action Taken:** No proactive outreach was made -- case was deprioritized as "survey only, no ticket."

**Outcome:** Churned the following month. Retrospective note: low satisfaction scores without an open ticket were being under-weighted at the time.

---

## 3. Competitor Offer / Switching

### Case 10

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 5 months tenure, fiber internet, streaming bundle.

**Flagged Reason:** Customer mentioned a named competitor's promotional price directly in a support chat.

**Action Taken:** Price-matched the competitor's offer for 6 months.

**Outcome:** Retained through the match period, but churned right after it expired -- the underlying price gap resurfaced.

---

### Case 11

**Customer Profile:** Two-year contract, 20 months tenure, DSL + phone.

**Flagged Reason:** Asked billing about early termination fees -- strong switching signal.

**Action Taken:** Offered to waive the remaining contract's early termination fee if they upgraded to fiber instead of leaving.

**Outcome:** Retained. Upgraded to fiber, higher-value customer post-intervention.

---

### Case 12

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 7 months tenure, fiber, no add-ons.

**Flagged Reason:** Neighbor/referral context noted in call log -- competitor was running a door-to-door promo in the area.

**Action Taken:** First offer was a generic retention discount, not tailored to the competitor's specific promo -- customer wasn't moved. Rep followed up by asking what the competitor was actually offering and matched the specific terms (price + contract length) instead of a blanket discount.

**Outcome:** Retained. Matching the actual competing offer, rather than a generic counter, closed the gap.

---

### Case 13

**Customer Profile:** One-year contract, 10 months tenure, fiber + streaming TV.

**Flagged Reason:** Explicitly told the rep they'd already signed up with a competitor, calling only to cancel.

**Action Taken:** No retention offer made -- treated as a lost cause given the explicit signup elsewhere.

**Outcome:** Churned. Correctly triaged as non-recoverable; no resources wasted on a doomed save attempt.

---

## 4. Life Change (Moving, Downsizing, No Longer Needs Service)

### Case 14

**Customer Profile:** Two-year contract, 18 months tenure, fiber + phone + multiple lines.

**Flagged Reason:** Called to ask about service transfer to a new address outside the coverage area.

**Action Taken:** Offered to hold the account/pause billing for 60 days in case the move fell through.

**Outcome:** Churned. Move was confirmed and permanent -- no fiber coverage at the new address. Correctly non-recoverable via any retention offer.

---

### Case 15

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 4 months tenure, DSL only.

**Flagged Reason:** Mentioned downsizing/moving to a smaller apartment during a support call.

**Action Taken:** Offered a lower-tier, cheaper plan suited to a smaller household instead of a discount on the existing plan.

**Outcome:** Retained. Downgraded plan matched their new situation; stayed on as a lower-revenue but retained customer.

---

### Case 16

**Customer Profile:** One-year contract, 6 months tenure, fiber + streaming movies.

**Flagged Reason:** Called about adding a temporary hold due to extended travel.

**Action Taken:** Set up a temporary service pause rather than losing the customer to cancellation.

**Outcome:** Retained. Resumed service after 2 months, tenure counted continuously.

---

## 5. Low Engagement / Underuse of Paid Features

### Case 17

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 5 months tenure, fiber + streaming TV + streaming movies, but streaming usage near zero per app logs.

**Flagged Reason:** Paying for two premium streaming add-ons with almost no usage -- classic "paying for something they don't use" risk.

**Action Taken:** An initial proactive email highlighting unused features alone didn't move the needle. Follow-up call offered a modest discount on one of the two streaming add-ons rather than just an awareness nudge.

**Outcome:** Retained. Dropped one add-on at a reduced price on the other; usage-cost mismatch was resolved rather than ignored.

---

### Case 18

**Customer Profile:** One-year contract, 8 months tenure, fiber + online security + device protection, low support contact overall (a quiet/disengaged account).

**Flagged Reason:** No support tickets, no logins to account portal in 60+ days -- "silent" risk profile rather than an active complaint.

**Action Taken:** Outbound call to check satisfaction, uncovered the customer had simply forgotten which add-ons they were paying for.

**Outcome:** Retained. Trimmed one unused add-on, customer appreciated the transparency, tenure continued.

---

### Case 19

**Customer Profile:** Month-to-month, 2 months tenure, DSL + online backup, minimal service usage.

**Flagged Reason:** New customer who never activated the online backup add-on they signed up for.

**Action Taken:** No proactive outreach -- flagged internally as low priority given short tenure and low spend.

**Outcome:** Churned. Retrospective note: early-tenure low-engagement accounts were being deprioritized, possibly missing an easy save window.
